## Decision

Kimi's two-gate proposal is **accepted with a necessary modification**, but it does not yet
authorize a candidate, implementation, report opening, or FINAL access.

The admissible sequence is:

```text
legal-select mechanism diagnosis
    -> freeze exactly one structural candidate
    -> viewed-report kill-only compatibility audit
    -> separately preregistered one-shot fresh confirmation
```

The missing item is the structural candidate.  Current evidence does not justify one.

## Q1 — Is the viewed-report necessary-condition gate selection leakage?

It is not fresh evaluation evidence, and it must not be called a legal selection or promotion
gate.  The 51,057 report attacks have already been viewed and already influenced the statement of
the CKDC constraint.  They are now development evidence.

They may nevertheless be used as a **kill-only retrospective compatibility audit** under all of
the following conditions:

1. exactly one candidate identity and complete functional form are frozen before its report
   behavior is computed;
2. the report artifact identity and denominator are frozen;
3. every report attack is evaluated and any hard-to-normal flip closes the route;
4. failure does not permit a revised rule, threshold, feature, exception, or second candidate;
5. passing creates no scientific claim and supplies no positive evidence;
6. passing only permits a request to preregister a separate one-shot test on genuinely untouched
   material; it does not itself authorize FINAL access.

Thus the answer is **MODIFY**: the proposed gate is legitimate as a falsification screen, not as a
clean selector.  The eventual untouched result would carry the confirmatory claim.

## Q2 — Can the correction be structural and parameter-free?

Not yet.  The obvious parameter-free rule,

```text
P2 hard AND M7 hard
```

is exactly conflict suppression by Boolean conjunction.  It is the previously rejected
degenerate fusion shape: when P2 and M7 disagree, it trusts M7 without evidence that the conflict
is benign.  Renaming it a bounded correction does not create a new mechanism.

The 4,986 legal benign conflicts are sufficient to establish that a useful benign population
exists, but they cannot identify the missing attack-side boundary because the dangerous quadrant
contains zero attacks.  A learned cut would therefore be one-sided and unvalidated.  A
parameter-free cut chosen after inspecting viewed report attacks would merely encode the viewed
report partition.

A structural candidate becomes admissible only if a prior audit identifies a **global normality
certificate** that is more specific than `M7 normal`, has a causal functional definition using
already-frozen signals, and is not observationally equivalent to P2-AND-M7 on the legal universe.
No such certificate has yet been demonstrated.

## Q3 — Does M7's hydraulic behavior have a safe mechanistic explanation?

No, not from current evidence.

The frozen M7 rule is:

```text
M7 hard = FrozenCKBQ hard AND tail_process_score >= 0.971323
```

In practice its attack rescue threshold is inactive.  M7 therefore revokes FrozenCKBQ alarms when
the 51-D process scorer looks benign.  This suppresses hydraulic very strongly, but the same
mechanism also suppresses low-volume stealth attacks: future-query recall falls to 63.19%, and
Merlin C&C falls to 4.5%.  Tail-margin and plain-CE arms behave similarly.  These facts show a
generic, aggressive process-normality filter; they do not show that M7 recognizes a hydraulic
protocol, long-session class, or other benign mechanism.

Consequently, `M7 is correct on hydraulic` is an empirical observation, not yet an interpretable
certificate that may safely override P2.

## Required final diagnosis before any candidate

The only justified next CKDC step is a bounded **M7 suppression-provenance audit** on existing,
non-FINAL artifacts.  It must be frozen before execution and must not train, select thresholds,
open PCAPs, or use FINAL.  It should mechanically decompose conflict rows by:

- FrozenCKBQ hard/normal state and frozen branch/reason;
- tail-process score relative to the already-frozen normal threshold;
- P2 state and source group;
- legal-select benign conflicts versus viewed-diagnostic hydraulic and viewed-diagnostic attacks,
  with the viewed partitions explicitly barred from candidate selection.

The audit asks one question only: does a pre-existing conjunction of frozen evidence define a
non-degenerate normality certificate, or is M7's apparent success indistinguishable from generic
alarm suppression?

Outcomes:

- **NO_CERTIFICATE:** CKDC fusion closes.  Do not train, do not invent another rule, and do not
  reopen external-data acquisition.
- **CERTIFICATE_CANDIDATE:** freeze exactly one rule, then perform the kill-only viewed-report
  audit above.  A pass would still require a separately authorized untouched one-shot test.
