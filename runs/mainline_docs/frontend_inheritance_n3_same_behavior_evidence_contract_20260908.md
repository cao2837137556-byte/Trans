# N3 repair and inherited-behavior evidence contract

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: N3_EVIDENCE_CONTRACT_FROZEN_NO_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY.
This companion governs interpretation of the proposed N3 conservative repair.
It grants no input access, implementation, fitting, confirmation or deployment.

## 1. Two separate deliverables

N3 can establish a finite-set repair of a fixed representation. It cannot by
itself establish broader inherited behavior. These must be reported separately
from the start, even if the repair is numerically successful.

| Evidence account | Available or proposed evidence | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Old incumbent | Unchanged historical system and its existing records | Its recorded results stand in their original scope |
| N1 training feasibility | 6,914 targets exact plus float32; all 8,660 correct in float32 | Fixed terminal representation admits the recorded training witness |
| N2 exposed development | One completed fixed-candidate check, with 846 unchanged protected errors | That candidate did not repair these development protections |
| N3 proposed joint repair | 12,711 predeclared fit constraints; canonical outcomes for all 13,866 targets | Only a verified exact/float32 witness establishes this conservative finite repair |
| Broader inherited behavior | No qualifying reserve identified in the current catalog | Remains unproved regardless of N3 outcome |
| New-device commissioning | No new protocol or run here | Remains downstream and untested |

N1's additional 1,746 originally accompanying training targets were not given
N1 exact certificates. N3 would newly constrain and exact-check them. Do not
retroactively expand the historical N1 claim.

## 2. New development-learning roles

The original F5 partition remains a historical identity. Under a future N3 real
authorization, a new role column declares the following use without renaming
the historical split or overwriting any F5/N1/N2 artifact:

- 8,660 original train targets: original_train_retention, all labels constrained.
- 1,484 original development protected targets: development_protection_fit.
- 2,567 N2-released B0N targets: development_B_release_retention_fit.
- 1,153 other development B0N plus 2 development A0W: exposed_report_only.

Thus 4,051 original development targets enter new fitting constraints and 1,155
remain report-only. The 2,567 targets are outcome-informed by the sealed N2
predictions. Freezing the rule before N3 prevents selection within N3; it does
not make the earlier N2 observation prospective, unseen or independent.

The report-only rows have already been exposed and can share contexts, events
or prefixes with fitted targets. Their labels may be read for the prescribed
report but never enter the LP, normalization, threshold choice or candidate
selection. Events serving fitted targets' causal prefixes are model inputs;
there is no claim of isolation merely because a target is report-only. Do not
call the residual rows a holdout, an independent validation set, or evidence of
successful unseen-family or unseen-device transfer.

## 3. What broader inheritance would require

A future evidence proposal must supply, before a new confirmation use:

1. The named old capability and its behavior correspondence, supported by
   provenance and task semantics. A common attack label, a renamed family or
   protocol equality is insufficient. A mismatch in literal family names is
   also not proof that underlying behavior can never overlap.
2. Candidate identity and exclusion of the evidence from fitting or selection
   of every learned component: encoder, head_hidden and final head, including
   relevant pretraining, normalization/calibration and repeated model selection.
   Any unverifiable pretraining overlap must be disclosed rather than assumed
   absent. Freezing only the final head does not undo encoder exposure.
3. Source/member/session dependencies, temporal cutoffs and exposure history.
   Distinct semantic contexts or coarse device_family values do not establish
   independent devices or sessions. Unknown dependencies remain unknown.
4. A label/provenance account and complete prospective inclusion rule; no
   error-selected confirmation subset. Previously inspected but fit-excluded
   evidence may support a labeled development test, never untouched confirmation.
5. Predeclared outcomes, denominators, candidate count, stop rules and the
   limits on claims warranted by the available independent units.

Current catalog: F5 development has three A Ingress Tool Transfer targets in one
context and three B File Download targets in two contexts. Neither recorded
family occurs in F5 training. Old F1 development's 2,000 reconnaissance targets
are also exposed, family-unmatched and outside N3. No suitable same-behavior,
fit-excluded reserve with established dependencies has been identified.

Changing an existing split after encoder fitting cannot establish fit exclusion.
This contract authorizes no additional data search, download, raw-capture scan,
normal_2/select/viewed/report/FINAL access or old F1 numeric evaluation. Missing
evidence limits the current conclusion; it is not permission to open a reserve.

## 4. Required reporting and decisions

Every N3 report must show exact and actual float32 results separately, the full
training retention result, all six development attacks with context/family,
all protected benign results, retained B target and context membership, original
per-key floors and the complete report-only outcome table. Do not hide a
protected failure behind aggregate accuracy or B utility.
The frozen B correct target/context sets must be subsets of the candidate
normal sets; equality is not required. Additional B releases are allowed and
reported without changing the frozen retention membership.

If the conservative repair succeeds, retain a research witness and state
broader_inheritance=NOT_PROVED. A real-affine negative certificate only concerns
the fixed cached 64D vectors and stronger conservative condition; it does not
exclude the weaker original B floors, rounding-dependent float32 classifiers,
head_hidden changes or the shared-model family. Unknown is not infeasibility.
The certificate's support is not necessarily a minimal conflicting set and
does not identify missing raw information or the historical failure cause.

All N3 outcomes leave F5 NO_ELIGIBLE_CHECKPOINT, N1/N2 permanently closed, the
incumbent unchanged, and K/deployment/new-device execution unauthorized.
