# Claim Boundary

## 可以说

- Enhanced LOW-GUARD+ will be compared under a three-layer fairness protocol。
- Adapter-level baselines share the same source_rich_top64 input and support budget。
- Method-level baselines receive supervision consistent with their learning setting。
- All methods share the same 1% OOD validation threshold protocol。
- Issue25b is a protocol and fairness design step, not an experimental result.

## 不能说

- Baselines have already been beaten。
- External validity has been proven。
- Semi-supervised baselines are weaker by definition。
- Full fairness means identical input for every method。
- Strong baseline results are known before issue25c。
- Enhanced LOW-GUARD+ is final without strong baseline execution。
