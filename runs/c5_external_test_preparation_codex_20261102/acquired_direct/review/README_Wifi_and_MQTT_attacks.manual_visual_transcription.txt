This Folder contains all the original Wi-Fi and MQTT traffic collected in .pcap files. In our study, the pcap files are divided into two groups: train and test. The train group is used for ML training, while the test group is used to evaluate the ML model. The features of the collected .pcap files are converted to CSV using TCPDUMP, and DPKT (See paper for more details), and are stored under their respective test and train subdirectories.
